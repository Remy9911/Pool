Pool Party

2–8 PLAYERS · PHONES AS CONTROLLERS
PLAY ON AIRCONSOLE
Opens AirConsole in this browser. Chrome on a laptop works best.
  1. 1
    Big screen: connect the laptop to the TV (HDMI), press Play above and go fullscreen with F11. Click once on the page to turn on sound.
  2. 2
    Phones: everyone opens airconsole.com (or the AirConsole app) and types the code shown on the TV.
  3. 3
    Play: vote a mode, tap READY. Tap around your float to splash — you shoot the opposite way. Last float in the pool wins the round; first to 5 wins takes the match.
